# Niña Blanca cover

One **16:9** still (`2560x1440`). Photoreal, modern, tied to **this** article’s scene (water piece = glass of water; flowers piece = white flowers).

**Never:** skulls, skeletons, bones, Grim Reaper, Santa Muerte statue faces, sugar skulls, Halloween, gore, readable text, logos.

Prefer: linen, water, wax, white roses, hands, a room at dusk.

Save `hero-16x9.jpg` in the run folder. After Approve + 7pm publish, copy to `Nina_Blanca/public/journal/<slug>.jpg` (keep under ~400KB). Set `cover: /journal/<slug>.jpg`.

No 4:5 social crops. This site has no social posting.
