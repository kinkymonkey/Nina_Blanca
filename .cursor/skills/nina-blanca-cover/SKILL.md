# Niña Blanca journal stills

**16:9 only** (`2560x1440`). One cover (`hero-16x9.jpg`) plus **3 or 4** body stills (`section-1-16x9.jpg` … `section-4-16x9.jpg`). Photoreal, modern, tied to **this** article’s scene (red robe piece = a red candle at a street shrine; history piece = devotees in Tepito). No 4:5. No Stories crop.

**Never:** skulls, skeletons, bones, Grim Reaper, Santa Muerte statue faces, sugar skulls, Halloween, gore, readable text, logos.

Prefer: real adults in real places (a street shrine, a market stall, a home altar) and candles or cloth in the color the article is about.

Save stills in the run folder. After Approve + 7pm publish, copy the cover to `Nina_Blanca/public/journal/<slug>.jpg` and body stills to `Nina_Blanca/public/journal/<slug>-section-N.jpg`. Set `cover: /journal/<slug>.jpg`. Body markers become markdown images on the site.

This site has no social posting.
