Death is already in the room when the machines are loud. You do not have to dress it up. You have to stay.

## What to put on the table

One white candle if the ward allows fire — often it does not. Then: water in a cup, a name said out loud, a hand that does not film the last hour.

## What not to post

A dying face is not an offering to the feed. If you need to ask the wall, ask without turning the family into a caption.

## If you are the one who waits

You may be angry. You may be bored. Both are ordinary. The White Mother is petitioned for company, not for a plot twist. See the sickroom prayer in the [library](/prayers).
