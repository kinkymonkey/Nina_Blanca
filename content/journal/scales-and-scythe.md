Statues accumulate props. Shops will sell you all of them. You do not need a catalog to pray.

## The scythe

It cuts what will not leave by polite request: vanity, delay, a story you tell to stay cruel. It is not a license to cut a neighbor.

## The scales

Fairness is not a slogan. Death does not keep a guest list. If you want the scales to tilt only for you, you have misunderstood the job.

## The globe and the lamp

The world is already hers. The lamp is company at night, not a spotlight. For the robes and their jobs, read [Aspects & Colors](/colors-and-aspects).
