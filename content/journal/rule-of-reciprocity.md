People talk as if Holy Death were a fence for curses. That talk sells. It is also how you wreck a house.

## What you give

Water. Time. A mouth that does not make a smaller world. Thanks when something already moved. That is reciprocity. It is not a contract with fine print in blood.

## After the ask

If the fever broke, say so. If it did not, do not punish the table. Stay decent. Come back tomorrow.
