Long before cameras discovered a skull, people kept a white cloth, a glass of water, and a name for Holy Death that could sit by a bed. That work was quiet. It still is, if you let it be.

This house stays with the white robe on purpose. Not because other colors are fake. Because a sanctuary that tries to be every current at once becomes a shop.

## What white is for

White is washing. A room that can be slept in. A fever that is tended without a show. A death that has a witness. If you need a hired ruin, you are in the wrong chapel.

## What we will not pretend

News likes a skull next to a crime scene. That picture is real for some people and a lie when it is sold as the whole religion. Nurses, migrants, mothers, and night workers keep her too. Crime is not a sacrament.

We are not the Vatican. We are not a rank you buy. If you need a living godparent, find one in the flesh.
