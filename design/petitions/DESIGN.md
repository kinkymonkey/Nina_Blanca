## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital sanctuary: quiet, respectful, and solemnly beautiful. Dedicated to devotion, study, and shared reverence, the aesthetic honors tradition while remaining contemporary and deliberate.

### Persona & Mood
- **Atmospheric & Reverent:** Evoking a darkened chapel illuminated by flickering votive candles. Deep charcoal backgrounds contrast with bone-white typography and muted antique gold highlights.
- **Contemplative & Unhurried:** Generous negative space, deliberate type pacing, and low visual noise invite quiet reflection.
- **Dignified & Grounded:** Firmly avoids sensationalism, macabre tropes, or gothic exaggeration. The interface embodies the solemn grace of sacred geometry and traditional altars.

### Design Movement
- **Atmospheric Minimalist Devotion:** A synthesis of strict structural restraint and tactile, candlelit warmth. Surfaces feature faint, low-contrast luminous glows, precise hairline borders, and deeply layered charcoal tiers that give the space physical gravity.

## Colors

The color scheme is structured around low-intensity warmth and sacred contrast, maintaining accessibility against deep, warm charcoal surfaces.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#111111`):** Near-black charcoal. Grounding, void-like foundation that isolates foreground devotional elements.
- **Layer 1 / Secondary Background (`#1B1918`):** Warm black, offering a subtle ambient lift for page partitions, navigation bars, and footers.
- **Layer 2 / Card Surface (`#221F1D`):** Primary interactive and content containers.
- **Layer 3 / Elevated Surface (`#2A2624`):** Modals, flyouts, and active states.

### Sacred Accents & Metadata
- **Antique Gold (`#B08A45` - Primary):** Reserved for primary interactive accents, sacred icons, active navigation tabs, and focal devotional actions.
- **Deep Burgundy (`#7A1F2B` - Secondary):** Used sparingly for ceremonial callouts, profound states, and subtle focal backgrounds behind iconography.
- **Candle Ivory (`#D9CCB6` - Tertiary):** Soft borders, meditative tags, and fine structural lines.

### Text & Neutral Hierarchy
- **Bone White (`#F1EBDD`):** Primary text. High legibility without the harshness of pure white against deep dark tones.
- **Ash Gray (`#B6AEA3`):** Secondary text, metadata, field labels, and secondary icon fills.
- **Muted Stone (`#6E675F`):** Disabled states, fine structural hairline rules, and tertiary timestamps.

## Typography

The type system pairs the classical literary gravitas of **EB Garamond** with the clear, humanist legibility of **Source Sans 3**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Conveys quiet dignity and time-tested reverence. Used for page titles, ceremonial declarations, quote excerpts, and prayer headings. It should remain uncompressed and never set in heavy weights; the standard 400 to 500 weights maintain a liturgical grace.
- **Body Text (Source Sans 3):** Delivers clean readability for historical essays, prayers, ritual instructions, and forum discussions. Avoids decorative flourishes to maximize reading comfort in low-light environments.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Source Sans 3 Caps):** Set in small, tracked-out uppercase (0.08em to 0.12em) to resemble architectural inscriptions on stone or bronze plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon architectural alignment, intentional pauses, and structured clarity. Content feels anchored like ceremonial tablets rather than transient web feeds.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width of 1200px centered canvas, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) lateral margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, 20px gutters, and 32px lateral margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) canvas margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
Vertical flow mimics the cadence of responsive prayer. Major thematic sections are separated by expansive intervals (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`), allowing each passage or devotional object to stand on its own without cognitive crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows. Instead, depth is articulated through surface layering, luminous candlelight glows, and delicate gilded borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Sanctuary Void - `#111111`):** Base backdrop. Unadorned, deep, absorbing.
- **Level 1 (Ground Surfaces - `#1B1918`):** Navigation bars and section shelves. Separated from the base solely by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Shrine Containers & Cards - `#221F1D`):** Devotional entries, texts, community topics. Encased in a `1px solid rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.18)` perimeter. 
- **Level 3 (Focused & Elevated Dialogs - `#2A2624`):** Modals and offerings. Retains a subtle ambient warmth beneath: `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.08)`.

### Candlelight Luminosity
Hover states, active focus markers, and devotional highlights utilize a soft, directional warm bloom rather than harsh outlines: `0 0 16px rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, establishing a carved-stone sensibility. Elements possess subtle softened corners that avoid digital sharpness without lapsing into playful, bubbly forms.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, tags, and small cards.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to major altar cards, prayer reading panels, and imagery viewports.
- **Capsule / Circular Forms:** Exclusively reserved for avatar medallions, ceremonial candle icons, and circular votive controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Votive Gold):** Solid `#B08A45` background with deep charcoal text (`#111111`), semi-bold weight. Hover introduces a slow 300ms transition to a warm candle glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Solemn Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.25)`, text `#F1EBDD`. Hover transitions border to `#B08A45` and subtle fill `#1B1918`.
- **Devotional / Ceremonial (Burgundy Deep):** Background `#7A1F2B`, text `#F1EBDD`, border `1px solid rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.3)`. Used for major intentions, petition submissions, and dedication acts.

### Cards & Sanctuary Tablets
- Structured in `#221F1D` with a top 1px border accent (`rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.3)`) and subtle outer stroke (`rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.06)`).
- Inner padding follows `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal dividers utilize soft tapering hairline borders that fade toward card edges.

### Chips & Sacred Tags
- Background `#1B1918`, text `#B6AEA3`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.12)`.
- Letter-spacing set to `0.08em` uppercase for ritual categorization (e.g., *WHITE VEIL*, *PRAYER*, *HISTORICAL RECORD*).
- Selected state adopts a thin Antique Gold border with `#F1EBDD` text.

### Inputs & Devotional Textareas
- Background `#181615`, text `#F1EBDD`, placeholder `#6E675F`.
- Rest border: `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.15)`.
- Focus border: `1px solid #B08A45`, backed by a warm candlelight aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.15)`). Zero native browser focus ring.

### Lists & Liturgies
- Borderless rows separated by `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.06)`.
- Hover introduces a gentle shift to `#1E1B19` accompanied by an Antique Gold left-rail marker (2px wide).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base: Unchecked box or circle in `#1B1918` with `1px solid rgba(217, 204, 182, 0.3)`.
- Checked: `#B08A45` background with an inset `#111111` indicator mark.

### Specialized Component: The Votive Offering Trigger
- A custom interactive prayer element: a stylized candle or flame indicator that devotees can touch or click to "light a candle."
- Activation transitions the flame icon from dimmed ash (`#6E675F`) to a radiant antique gold with an animated radial pulse (`rgba(176, 138, 69, 0.4)`), incrementing a solemn prayer counter.
